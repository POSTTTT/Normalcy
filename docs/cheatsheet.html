Normalcy Cheatsheet

Default triggers. Rename any of them in Settings.

Open the bar ⌥Space

In the bar

ReturnRun the command
⇧ReturnRun it, keep the bar open
Tabor⌃NNext in the list
⇧Tabor⌃PPrevious in the list
←↓↑→Same as h j k l
EscClear, or close when empty
⌃UClear what you typed
⌃WDelete the last word

Apps

oappOpen or launch an app
bappSwitch to a running appMatches the start of the name: b saf
bhjklNearest window that way

Desktops and tabs

d2Go to desktop 2 (1–9)
dh/lDesktop on the left / right
m2Move window to desktop 2, follow it
mh/lMove window left / right a desktop
tMove tab to its own window
th…and put it on the left halfAny position works: t l, t f, t hk

Size and scroll

fFill the screen
cCenter the window
+/-10% bigger / smaller, centered
rel/hMove the free edge right / leftA window against the right side moves its left edge, so re h grows it
rej/kMove the free edge down / upRepeat for more: re ll. The window beside it makes room
sjkhlScroll as you press, no ReturnEsc stops
setOpen Settings
